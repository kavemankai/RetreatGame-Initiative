## 2026-10-14: playtest 1 fixes, overwatch reactions (Phase 4 core) added
- **Playtest feedback (owner):** order and pace fine, controls fine; the AI seemed to do nothing; show shot data before confirming; sandbox AP too high.
- **Cause of the idle AI:** only the patrol ("aggressor") advances; guards and sentries hold and their one tool, overwatch, was switched off. Enemy turns in the fog also logged nothing.
- **Overwatch reactions:** `ActionResolver._react` / `watchers_covering`. Armed for 4 AP (not refunded). After each step a mover takes, every armed enemy whose cone, weapon range, sight and line cover the new tile fires once, free, in initiative order then id; its charge is spent. A killed mover stops (path cancelled); a surviving mover resumes; a shaken/broken/dead watcher cannot react; reactions never chain (nothing listens to reaction shots). Shot events carry `reaction: true`. The ledger charges the mover only what it spent. Sentries start armed; `ActivationAI` arms when its plan says so and then holds. Route preview flags steps covered by a visible watcher's cone ("!"). Overwatch button is back ("Overwatch (4 AP)").
- **UI:** the shot confirm message now shows target, hit chance, damage, range, AP cost and AP left (and a label on the target tile, not yet seen rendered); the log says "The enemy acts out of sight." when enemies acted but nothing was visible (no positions or counts leaked).
- **Sandbox:** "AP per activation" button (8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20) feeds the activation engine only; default is `data/combat/ap_costs.json` (14).
- **Tests:** 428 tests, 2089 checks, 0 failed (`tests/test_reactions.gd` adds 9 plus an AP setting test). Mutation-checked: one-shot consumption, shaken/broken watchers. The explicit "stop after the mover dies" is redundant with dead units having zero AP; kept as a guard.
- **Still open:** no new APK built since playtest 1; human playtest of overwatch; AI quality beyond arming (Phase 3 decision log, repeat detector, 100-battle soak); reaction on-device feel.
