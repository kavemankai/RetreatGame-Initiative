## 2026-10-14: Phase 3 done (enemy AI quality, diagnostics, 100-battle soak)
- **Gate result:** `tools/soak_combat.gd -- --battles=100`: 100 of 100 battles finish (avg 4.7 rounds), **0 illegal actions, 0 frozen turns, 0 invariant breaks, 0 oscillations**, 2 idle activations with a target in range out of 2,767 (was 413 of 2,066 before the fixes, with 9 of 20 battles stalled). p95 plan time 45 ms on this PC (not measured on a phone yet). Stale plan steps dropped (not sent): 258; activations ended by the replan cap: 268 (about 10%, harmless but worth a look).
- **Cause of the stalls found by the soak:** both sides sat on good cover tiles because nothing rewarded moving to a tile with a clear shot lane; and objective steering used straight-line distance so units wedged behind walls.
- **Fixes:** `SimAIProfile.w_lane` (new, default 0; 8 on `default` and `aggressor` in `data/ai_profiles.json`; guards and sentries still hold): bonus per visible enemy (max 2) with a clear uncovered shot to the tile. Objective distance is now a walking-distance field computed once per plan (`SimAI._objective_distance`), with a straight-line fallback. All 428 legacy tests still pass unchanged.
- **Diagnostics:** `SimAI.plan_for(..., diag)` out-parameter (scores per option, never changes the plan). `ActivationAI` (now per team) keeps counters (activations, plans, idle, idle with target, caps, oscillations, dropped, rejected, p95 plan ms) and, with `keep_log`, a decision log of plan, act, drop, end (reason) and oscillation rows. `CombatSoak` (`game/combat_soak.gd`) builds seeded 4v4 and 4v8 battles on the proving ground with random kinds, roles, hollows and positions, runs the AI on both sides (the squad side with `scout` on, a stand-in human that knows where the enemy is; never on for the AI under test), and checks invariants after every command.
- **Tests:** 440 tests, 2130 checks, 0 failed (`tests/test_ai_scenarios.gd` adds 12: open-field advance, wall detour, cover stand-off, low AP, stale step, unreachable target, two aggressors, fog, decision log, oscillation detector, determinism, 6-battle soak). No memory of last-seen targets (units that are shot from the fog do not react to the shot). Not covered yet: retreat at low health, reload (no ammo model), AI shared intel, human sanity playtest of the new behaviour.

## 2026-10-14: playtest 1 fixes, overwatch reactions (Phase 4 core) added
- **Playtest feedback (owner):** order and pace fine, controls fine; the AI seemed to do nothing; show shot data before confirming; sandbox AP too high.
- **Cause of the idle AI:** only the patrol ("aggressor") advances; guards and sentries hold and their one tool, overwatch, was switched off. Enemy turns in the fog also logged nothing.
- **Overwatch reactions:** `ActionResolver._react` / `watchers_covering`. Armed for 4 AP (not refunded). After each step a mover takes, every armed enemy whose cone, weapon range, sight and line cover the new tile fires once, free, in initiative order then id; its charge is spent. A killed mover stops (path cancelled); a surviving mover resumes; a shaken/broken/dead watcher cannot react; reactions never chain (nothing listens to reaction shots). Shot events carry `reaction: true`. The ledger charges the mover only what it spent. Sentries start armed; `ActivationAI` arms when its plan says so and then holds. Route preview flags steps covered by a visible watcher's cone ("!"). Overwatch button is back ("Overwatch (4 AP)").
- **UI:** the shot confirm message now shows target, hit chance, damage, range, AP cost and AP left (and a label on the target tile, not yet seen rendered); the log says "The enemy acts out of sight." when enemies acted but nothing was visible (no positions or counts leaked).
- **Sandbox:** "AP per activation" button (8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20) feeds the activation engine only; default is `data/combat/ap_costs.json` (14).
- **Tests:** 428 tests, 2089 checks, 0 failed (`tests/test_reactions.gd` adds 9 plus an AP setting test). Mutation-checked: one-shot consumption, shaken/broken watchers. The explicit "stop after the mover dies" is redundant with dead units having zero AP; kept as a guard.
- **Still open:** no new APK built since playtest 1; human playtest of overwatch; AI quality beyond arming (Phase 3 decision log, repeat detector, 100-battle soak); reaction on-device feel.
